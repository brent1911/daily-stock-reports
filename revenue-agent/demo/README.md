# Sales demo

A single self-contained page showing what the product does, built to be opened
on a phone in front of a gym owner.

**Live:** https://claude.ai/artifact/PWdqp1Zr189BFLrks4uT2w
*(private — share it from the page's Share menu before sending it to anyone)*

## What it shows

1. **The headline** — revenue recovered this month against what the service costs.
2. **Where it came from** — the four leaks, each with real counts behind it.
3. **Watch one happen** — press a button and the agent works a declined card in
   front of you, from the 6am Stripe failure to the money clearing at 11:20.
4. **What we chose not to count** — money that came back that we are *not*
   billing for. This is the section that sells the thing.

## Notes

- All figures are **sample data**, modelled on a 384-member studio at $149/month,
  and marked as such on the page. Nothing here is a real gym.
- The numbers follow the conservative attribution rule in
  [`../docs/attribution.md`](../docs/attribution.md) — the demo and the database
  tell the same story on purpose.
- No build step, no dependencies. Open the file, or send the link.

## Before showing it to a real prospect

Swap the sample figures for that gym's own numbers if you can get them —
their member count, their plan price, their failed-payment rate. A demo built
on a prospect's real billing data closes considerably better than a generic one.
