How a room gets made

Four stages, in order. You approve each one before we move.

Stage 01

Intake

Photos, measurements, light direction, and the honest list of what you already own and want to keep.

Stage 02

Direction

The board lands: palette, materials, layout. You mark what stays and what we push further.

Stage 03

Sourcing

Every piece specified, priced at trade where we have it, and tracked to your door on one sheet.

Stage 04

Install

Placement, styling, and the last ten percent — the part that makes the room read as finished.
