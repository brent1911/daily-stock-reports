The studio
Most homes don't need a renovation. They need a point of view — a palette that holds, three pieces moved, and one light changed from cold to warm.

We work room by room, in a narrow range of materials chosen to age well: lime plaster, unbleached linen, rift-sawn oak, honed stone, brass left to darken on its own.

Founder & stylist

Kieve Bourgeois

I started Still Studio for the houses that are almost there — good bones, good intentions, and a living room nobody can quite settle in. The fix is rarely a renovation. It's a palette that agrees with itself and a few decisions made properly.

My work sits between a moodboard and a finished room: I set the direction, source it honestly at every price tier, and stay through install day. Nothing here has to be expensive to look considered. It has to be chosen.

— Kieve
