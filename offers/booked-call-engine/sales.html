Why ads don't work for most coaches

It's almost never the targeting. It's one of these four, and they compound:

Your offer can't afford traffic

A $150 month-to-month offer can afford about $32 per booked call. Nothing you can buy comes close to $32. No targeting, creative or budget decision rescues it — the structure has to change first. Most coaches never run this number.

Nothing is tracked, so nothing learns

If no conversion carries a value, Meta can't report a return and can't optimise toward revenue. Your account spends for weeks and learns almost nothing. This is a settings problem that takes twenty minutes and almost nobody does it.

The funnel doesn't match the budget

Meta needs roughly 50 conversion events a week to optimise. Run a call funnel at $40/day optimising for bookings and it will never leave the learning phase. You'll conclude call funnels don't work. What didn't work was the budget.

Half the booked calls never happen

Most coaches run at a 50% show rate and treat it as normal. Going to 75% cuts your cost per held call by a third and costs nothing in ad spend. It's the cheapest money in the entire funnel and it's sitting there untouched.

No. It starts at the account setup screen and names every field. If you've run ads before you'll move faster through sections 4 and 5, and the rest is the same either way.

A focused weekend gets you live. Then 90 days of running it before you know your real numbers — and the guide is honest that the first couple of weeks buy data, not clients.

What ad budget do I need?

$20/day for the DM funnel. $70–100/day minimum for the call funnel, for a reason explained in Section 3 — below that Meta can't optimise and the funnel genuinely won't work. If you've got $20/day, run the DM funnel. It's the better starting point anyway.
