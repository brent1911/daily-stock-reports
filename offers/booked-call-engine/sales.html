Why ads don't work for most coaches

Almost never the targeting. One of four things:

  • The offer can't afford traffic. A $150 month-to-month offer can afford about $32 per booked call. Nothing costs $32.
  • Nothing's tracked. No value on your conversions means Meta can't optimise toward revenue. Twenty-minute fix.
  • Wrong funnel for the budget. Meta needs ~50 events a week. A call funnel at $40/day never leaves the learning phase.
  • Half the calls never happen. A 50% show rate is normal. 75% cuts your real cost per call by a third, free.

Fix the offer. Work out what a call can cost. Build the funnel that fits. Make them turn up. In that order — I spent a long time doing the fourth one first.
