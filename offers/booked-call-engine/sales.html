Why ads don't work for most coaches

It's almost never the targeting. It's one of four things, and they compound:

  • The offer can't afford traffic. A $150 month-to-month offer can afford about $32 per booked call. Nothing you can buy costs $32.
  • Nothing is tracked, so nothing learns. If no conversion carries a value, Meta can't optimise toward revenue. Twenty-minute fix that almost nobody does.
  • The funnel doesn't match the budget. Meta needs ~50 events a week to optimise. Run a call funnel at $40/day and it never leaves the learning phase.
  • Half the booked calls never happen. Most coaches sit at a 50% show rate. Going to 75% cuts cost per held call by a third, at no extra ad spend.
