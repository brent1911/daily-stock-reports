## Why it is pure DIY

An earlier version was an 8-week cohort at $797 with weekly group calls. It
was dropped: 25.5 delivery hours per cohort is a ceiling, and it needed a
coordinator at 30 buyers and a second coach once cohorts overlapped.

This version has **zero marginal delivery cost**. One buyer or ten thousand,
the work is identical and already done. At $10k/month ad spend both models
land around 2x; above that the cohort version needs payroll and this one
needs nothing.

The trade is roughly $600/month less course revenue for 25.5 fewer hours per
cohort, no support ceiling and no hiring. Taken deliberately.

## The cost of going DIY, and how it is handled

Removing the humans removes the people who unblocked stuck buyers. Three
mechanisms replace them, all built once:

1. **Swipe-and-deploy, never "here's how to write it."** Every asset ships as
   something to copy with the editable parts bracketed. This removes the
   judgement calls that previously needed a coach.
2. **Decision trees instead of advice.** Anywhere the cohort version would
   have said "bring it to the call," there is now an if-then path.
3. **Troubleshooting as a first-class section.** Twenty failure modes with
   fixes, grouped by account/policy, tracking, delivery and conversion.

Known residual risks, to watch rather than assume away:

- DIY completion runs 5–15% against a cohort's 40–70%. Low completion means
  fewer finished buyers, so fewer testimonials and fewer agency leads.
- DIY refunds run 10–20% against 5–8%. Hence a 14-day guarantee gated on
  watching modules 1–3 rather than an open 30-day one.

## The two sections that carry the product

**Section 9, the show-up system.** The cheapest money in the funnel. Moving
50% → 75% show rate cuts cost per *held* call by a third at zero extra ad
spend. The lever is the 60-second SMS asking for a one-character reply — a
buyer who has typed "Y" to a person shows up materially more often. The
day-before message deliberately offers a reschedule, trading a few moved
calls for far fewer silent no-shows.

**Section 3, attaching a value to the booking event.** Sending `value:` with
the Schedule event (client value × show% × close%) is what makes a return
figure exist at all. Almost nobody does it, which is why so many coach
accounts report nothing in that column.

## Consistency with the Coach Offer System

Both products use `Ceiling = Show% × Close% × (Client value ÷ 3)` at 70%/30%,
and the same five structures and ceilings — $32 / $168 / $210 / $301 / $420.
The month-to-month $150 row is carried across because "this offer can afford
$32 per booked call" is the strongest single argument either product makes.

**If the ceiling numbers are ever revised, both products must change
together.** They are sold to the same buyer.

   exchange for a documented result, then rebuild the opening around those.
2. **Decide the hosting.** `index.html` is self-contained and uploads
   anywhere. For gated access, the GoHighLevel membership area is already

3. **The Creative Pack is not built.** The $97 bump is assumed in the funnel

   2.0x) but does not exist yet.
4. **Front-end traffic is the real dependency.** At 150 buyers/month this
   tier yields ~13.5 sales; at 15/month it yields one. The Meta account has
   had no data since 27 August 2026, so first sales come from the existing
   audience, not cold traffic.
